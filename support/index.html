FAQ

My widget is not updating

Widgets update on a schedule decided by iOS, so a change can take a short while to show. Open Yonder once to refresh them. If it still looks wrong, remove the widget from the Home Screen and add it again, or restart your iPhone.

I do not get notifications

Check that notifications are allowed for Yonder in iOS Settings > Notifications > Yonder, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not silencing them. Reminders are scheduled on your device at the time you chose in Yonder.

How does Pro work?

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Free use includes up to 3 active countdowns. Pro includes unlimited countdowns, the Pro themes, your own photo backgrounds and the photo widgets.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Yonder, go to Settings > Pro and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a countdown in the app to remove it and its photo, or delete the app to remove everything. Yonder stores nothing on any server. See the Privacy Policy.
